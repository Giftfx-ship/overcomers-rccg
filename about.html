About Us

RCCG Overcomers HOC - Oke Ado, Ogbomoso

Our Story

RCCG Overcomers HOC is a parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God located at Oke Ado, Old Stadium Road, Ogbomoso, Oyo State.

We are a community of believers committed to winning souls for Christ, raising disciples, and transforming lives through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our vision is to be a beacon of light in Ogbomoso and beyond, demonstrating God's love through worship, fellowship, and service.
